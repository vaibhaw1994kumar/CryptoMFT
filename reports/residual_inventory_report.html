1. Summary

Two capital/risk-layer gaps were fixed. (1) Residual exposure: a balanced two-leg package reserved zero residual capacity, the residual bound was checked per plan only, and a plan's PnL booked an unhedged leg's cash as profit — so recorded paper cycles ended holding up to 0.006–0.007 BTC naked against a 0.002 limit, and the headline paper PnL across 44 cycles read +698.91 USDT while the marked equity change was -25.46 USDT. (2) Spot inventory: a spot sell was admitted against the position limit only, so a venue holding no BTC could sell (on the synthetic stream BYBIT sold 0.080 BTC it never held). Both are fixed, tested and enabled in the platform configs.

3. Changes

3.1 Residual exposure (commit 40b52d4)

Admission (risk.py):
  planned_residual = max(|leg quantity|)                         # worst case, not the flat net
  refuse if |carried| ≥ max_residual_base                          → "carried residual at limit"
  refuse if reserved_residual + planned_residual + |carried|
            > max_legging_exposure_base (default max_position_base) → "max legging exposure exceeded"

Residual bound (oms.py):
  carried = Σ residual_base of CLOSED plans                        # derived from plans ⇒ restart-safe
  within  = residual == 0
            or ( |residual| < cap and ( |carried + residual| < cap or |carried + residual| ≤ |carried| ) )
  not within ⇒ HEDGE_UNWIND → unwind order, instead of closing with a naked leg

Marked PnL (oms.py, models.py):
  residual_mark_price = mean mid of the instrument's valid books (fallback: signal prices)
  net_pnl = gross_pnl + residual_base × residual_mark_price − fees − unwind_cost

Visibility: snapshot carried_residual_base, total_residual_base; monitoring CRITICAL on total_residual_base;
            analyze-session WARN "no_carried_residual"; config risk.max_legging_exposure_base.

6. Known gaps

  • Quote reservation excludes fees; unwind (risk-reducing) orders are not blocked by inventory.
  • A package's reservation is held until the plan closes, so between fill and close balances are counted twice (conservative).
  • No inter-venue transfer/rebalancing model (results labelled PRE_FUNDED_INVENTORY_NO_REBALANCING); long one-directional runs exhaust a venue.
  • Reservations are released at plan close, not reduced leg by leg as fills arrive.
  • Two multi-strategy plumbing tests in test_watch_pipeline.py pin unconstrained inventory because the synthetic stream drains BYBIT.
