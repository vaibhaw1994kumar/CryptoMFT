  • False fills are rare: 4/864 = 0.5% of real cancels under proportional, all on MNT (ADA 0 of 721). The simulator does not invent fills for orders that were cancelled.
  • Fill timing is consistent with the earlier study (83.9 % vs 80.5 %), but it is an upper bound: the public trade tape contains our own fills, so a really filled order always has its print in the data.
  • PostOnly fails on false rejects: the simulator refuses 2.7% of orders the exchange accepted (limit 2 %). It catches 37 of 38 real rejects.
  • FillModelV2 is not an improvement: its Brier score beats the base rate, but its probabilities are badly calibrated (slope 2.11); 2 May is a different regime (5 s fill rate 0.7% against 20.0% on the fit days).

What this does not establish

  • Spot, not perp; the perp check is 8 small orders.
  • Two coins.
  • One heavy cancel day: 839 of 864 cancels are on 2 May, which is also a different quoting regime.
  • One book source: the recorder capture of 19–25 April was lost, so there is no second, finer view of the book (the archive updates about every 100 ms; the recorder every 42 ms).
  • Fill timing is an upper bound (our own fills are in the tape).
  • The archive has no receive time of ours; the feed delay is the profile's assumption.
  • The cancel-model bounds are bounds, not measurements.
