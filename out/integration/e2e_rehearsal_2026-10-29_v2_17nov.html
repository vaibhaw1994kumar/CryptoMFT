13. Open items

  • Case 2i on v1_10 is NOT COVERED. The capture gives ONE real amend per run (a quote already 0.9 filled, at 11:01:23), so reject, timeout, disconnect and duplicate Filled each met that single amend. The paths that never met a real amend: a fill during AMEND_PENDING (met only in the timeout variant), cancels during a pending amend, and above all the disconnect variant ended with the amend timed out and NO venue query before the reconnect. What would cover it: (1) repeated injected hedge-venue moves timed to hit a quote that rests deep with slack, so there are many amends and not one (the dense injection of 2 ticks every 20 s met one); (2) an OMS-level test where an amend times out while the router still holds the request, which settles whether the deferred query is by design. (3) the full day with the pair-close switch on produced 23 real amends with no injection (section 9): the same reject, timeout, disconnect and duplicate-Filled variants run on THAT day would meet many amends; it is not v1_10 (the switch is separate), so it is named here as the scenario, not run. Until then `case2i_passed` stays false and every reprice is a cancel plus a fresh quote under real orders.
  • The loss stop and the two venues' mids. The day's low point of the A2 figure is at 20:43:16 UTC in every v1_10 run, with the Bybit mid 96.305 and the Binance mid 97.135 (86 bps apart) on a flat-net hedged pair: -1.59 USDT at 2 SOL per venue and -3.78 USDT at 4.7 SOL, against the -5 limit. Whether that moment is a real dislocation or one venue's book being wrong while still VALID was not traced. Either way the stop is marked at each venue's own mid, so a wide gap on a larger hedged position would trip the latched kill switch without any trading loss: the owner may want the mark rule or the limit reviewed before the gross per venue is raised.
  • Controller counter: in the close_pairs run the ledger records 23 AMEND_CONFIRMED and the reprice controller's own counter says 8. The ledger is the record; the counter's rule was not checked.
  • A9 target not met: the live path keeps up to about 3,000 to 3,500 events/s per venue, not 10,000 (see ORDER3_A9_FIX_RESULTS.md); with arrival stamps on, an overload now shows as book age and resyncs.
  • A3 clock source: a REST server-time probe from this Mac has an uncertainty of about 85 to 95 ms against the 5 ms limit, so the gate would hold quoting in every real-order run; the source or the limit is the owner's decision.
  • The rerun with derivatives.jsonl (recorded marks and funding) is still owed; the pair-close switch and the testnet drill (reduce-only acceptance and its exemption from the minimum notional) are unverified on a venue.
