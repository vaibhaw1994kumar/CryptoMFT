10. What this rehearsal does not show

  • No funding and no recorded marks (derivatives.jsonl for the day is pending; the margin check uses the labelled MARK_FROM_MID fallback).
  • One day only: no out-of-sample claim, regimes are the day's own terciles.
  • No own orders on the venues: queue position and fills are the simulator's (a fill needs a real print; nothing is trained on them).
  • The approved limits listed as NOT built could not bind.
  • Network and venue behaviour (ack latency, rate-limit responses) are the profile's modelled latencies, not measurements of the mainnet.
