## F06 aggregate dust cap: DONE

What was already built and not redone: the per-venue signed carry (`hedge_faults.dust_carry next_hedge`), the 60 s alarm, the restart recovery (`OMS._recover_dust_state`).

Built (core `oms.py`, `hedge_lifecycle.py`, `config.py`, `session.py`; switches absent = off):
- a. `hedge_faults.dust_max_notional_usdt` (v1_16: 50, ASSUMED owner placeholder) and `hedge_faults.dust_max_age_s` (v1_16: 300, ASSUMED). The carried balances are valued in USDT per direction over every venue and instrument; only the same instrument is netted across venues; mark = mid of a valid book of that instrument, else the last carried price (`OMS.dust_exposure_usdt`). At the bound (`OMS._dust_bound_check`): no new risk (the session asks no strategy for a signal, a quote strategy gets `dust_bound`), ONE alarm `DUST_BOUND_REACHED` with the alert context, the resting opening quotes withdrawn once (`DUST_BOUND_CANCEL`; hedges, unwinds and reduce-only closing quotes kept), what is sendable hedged at once (the ordinary dust-host chain), the rest reported once per balance (`DUST_BOUND_UNSENT`), summed in `DUST_BOUND_REMEDY`; `DUST_BOUND_CLEARED` when back under every bound.
- Difference from the order text, as reported before the build: with SOLUSDT on one hedge venue a balance never exceeds one minimum order (about 5 USDT on Binance), so the 50 USDT cap cannot be reached; the 300 s age bound is the one that binds. In a session the bound LATCHES (no new quote, so no fill to absorb the dust): it clears only by a price move that makes the balance sendable, an opposite fill, or a manual step. No maker-venue reduce of the dust is built (below Bybit's 0.1 SOL minimum for one instrument).
- b. Where the carried dust is counted (no arithmetic changed; the dust is an unhedged maker fill): the strategy net and gross exposure (`risk._exposure_values`, from FILL events), the margin engine (fills), the A2 daily-loss equity (`session._equity_at_mids`, ledger positions), the residual totals (`carried_dust_base`), and the end-of-day report (`hedge_dust_carry.bound`). The breaker's net-exposure timer exemption stays (`session._net_abs`). Test: `DustBound.test_the_carried_dust_is_in_the_strategy_net_exposure_and_the_ledger_position_but_not_in_the_breaker_timer_net`.
- c. Late fill after a kill. Where the code already does it: the A2 / A5 kills call `OMS.activate_kill_switch(keep_risk_reducing=True)`, which cancels the quote and keeps the plan open; the late fill runs `_hedge_for_maker_fill`; the hedge is risk-reducing and passes the kill switch (`LEGGING_CONTROL_OVERRIDE` `GLOBAL:kill_switch`). Tests: kill then Filled, kill then partial, kill then restart then late fill, the default kill. FOUND AND FIXED: when the venue CONFIRMS the cancel at a lower fill and reports the fill after (Bybit `cancel` in `testnet.py` reports CANCELLED with nothing filled when its order-state query is not yet visible), the plan closed and the later fill raised `closed plan is immutable` and was never hedged. New switch `hedge_faults.late_fill_after_close: reopen` (v1_16; default `refuse` = the old behaviour): the fill is booked once, `LATE_FILL_AFTER_CLOSE` alarm, the plan reopens (PARTIAL, persisted `PLAN_REOPENED_LATE_FILL`) and only the new part is hedged.

Tests (all pass): `tests/test_core_hedge_scenarios.py` `DustBound` (6), `LateFillAfterKill` (7); `tests/test_session_breaker.py` `F06DustBoundStopsNewQuotes` (1). Focused files: 387 passed.

## F05 per-pair close reservation: DONE

Built behind `arb.close_reservation` (default false; v1_16 writes `close_pairs: off` and `close_reservation: false` explicitly, so the production candidate does NOT close pairs) and the exit keys (inert in v1_16):
- a. Pair id = the entry plan's parent id. A closing plan reserves, at its creation, quantity on named pairs (oldest first, ASSUMED), each at most its closable quantity on both venues, in total at most the ledger position on each venue less every outstanding reservation (`OMS._close_allocate`, `CLOSE_RESERVED`); nothing closable: `CLOSE_NOTHING_CLOSABLE`, refused. Held until the closing plan is CLOSED, then the unused part is released (`CLOSE_RESERVATION_RELEASED`). Pair legs come from the fills (never stored): a restart rebuilds them, plus the allocation (`CLOSE_RESERVED` on the plan store), the closing-plan marker and the beyond-flat flag (`CLOSE_PAIR_ALARM`). The strategy sizes the closing quote from `OMS.closable_quantity` (the double count during a pending amend is gone with the switch on).
- b. Reduce-only by the venue position when the child is built: closing quote, closing hedge, unwinds (an unwind that re-opens the position is NOT reduce-only). An entry hedge is never reduce-only.
- c. Cases (`tests/test_core_hedge_scenarios.py` `PerPairCloseReservation`, both venues reconciled at the end, every child's reduce-only flag asserted): partial maker close then partial hedge close; partial maker close then FAILED hedge close (unwound on the maker venue, not reduce-only); a second exit while the first closes (capped, a third refused); a late entry fill during a close; a restart mid-close; inventory partly closed by hand (capped at the venue position). Off by default: nothing reserved.
- d. Exit rules (`ArbConfig`, ASSUMED values, off): `close_triggers` (edge default; time_stop, funding, inventory_limit, margin_limit, end_of_session rest the close at the maker touch, never aggressive), `max_pair_hold_s` 3600, `funding_close_lead_s` 300, `end_of_session_utc_s` off, `close_unfilled_after_s` (CLOSE_UNFILLED report, ASSUMED 60 when on). Tests: `tests/test_close_pairs.py` `F05ExitRules` (6).
- e. `CLOSE_PAIR_BEYOND_FLAT` = 0 in every case test. A labelled 29 Oct replay with `close_pairs` and `close_reservation` on (`f05_close_on.sh`, EXPLORATORY, 29 Oct waiver) is reported in the next update of this file.
