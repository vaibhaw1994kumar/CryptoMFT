Backtest and core integration: status

7 Nov 2026. One shared platform: rules live once in crypto_stat_arb, backtest and live call it. Every P&L figure here is EXPLORATORY (stated profile, size 1, days, assumed fees). HOLDOUT untouched.

Short version. The simulator baseline is frozen and proven (v1_2). The repricing feature the live bot has and the backtest lacked is built, modular and off. The accounting switch-over (Phase B2) has started: equity valuation, funding and margin now agree with the core to rounding in the shadow. Phases C to F and the strategy steps S1 to S3 are not started. The real strategy loses about 2 bps per fill after fees in the simulator, mostly because quotes are never repriced when Binance moves.

5. Built but off (modular, left for the trader or the owner)

  • Quote repricing (S0): rule in the core passive tactic (decide_reprice), target from the strategy's edge at the current Binance price (requote_target), simulator glue in new_backtest/requote.py, setting execution.primary.reprice, profile v1_3 with every number null and the rule disabled. Amend loses queue priority; the old order stays fillable until the amend lands (H30); PostOnly is checked at arrival. A smoke run with illustrative numbers ran end to end and exposed one bug (max-age re-quote repeating), since fixed. Needs the numbers.
  • Placement on the truth book: switch built, measured inert on 3 orders, left view.
  • Through-print rule: engine fixed (one print budget; a netted print still reaches it), off in v1_2 and off the freeze list.
  • Cohort queue: two fixes, sensitivity case only; refuses amend.

6. Not started, and what each needs first

  • Phase C (the biggest): one hedge lifecycle state machine in the core, one shared reject-code table, retry limits and a maximum residual duration, the seven tests through the core path and all 40 catalogue cases. Needs your decision on the two conflicts recorded as DECISION_PENDING_CALIBRATION (unwind band 15 vs 10 bps, residual limit 30 vs 5 s). p_fail and loss_fail_bps become SCENARIO values (not calibrated) until real execution logs exist.
  • Phase D, E, F: follow C; E includes flatten on breaker trip (missing in both stacks) and the lot and minimum checks (no SOLUSDT rules stored).
  • S1: the adverse-selection table, fitted on the repricing strategy's fills on TRAIN, validated on SELECT only, shipped as a new profile version.

8. Limits that travel with every number

  • March slices are 2 hours, Binance best bid/ask only, no funding file; stamps, latency preset and fees are ASSUMED.
  • Hidden liquidity ahead of us and RPI orders on both venues are not modelled.
  • The print-first leftover (a decrease larger than its print) is not order-independent (ratio below 1.2): a known limit.
  • Strategy-fill samples are tiny (24 January fills, 2 March fills); the probe (2,035 fills) is Bybit-only with a 15 s order life.
  • p_fail is a placeholder and loss_fail_bps comes from a simulated study.
