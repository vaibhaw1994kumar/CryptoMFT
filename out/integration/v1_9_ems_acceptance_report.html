1. What this report is, and what it is not

  • Replays run from frozen code snapshots, one job at a time, fixed seeds. The determinism check (one scenario run twice) is the first row of the matrix.
  • Regime boundaries (volatility, spread, liquidity terciles) of the 29 Oct runs are in-sample: the day's own terciles (the seven March training days are not on this machine). They describe that day; they are not an out-of-sample result. The March replay uses a PARTIAL training-slice file (2 hours of 03-01).
  • The spread tercile is nearly degenerate on March (one tick is almost every second), so its split carries little information.
  • Most cells hold fewer than 30 quotes, so they print counts and no rate.
  • Scenario windows are 2-hour cuts of the capture. The books are warmed from the capture's start (09:56 UTC) with quoting off until the window opens.

4. Findings

  1. A real defect, found by the 2d replay and fixed. With a 3 s ghost hedge outage a hedge chain moved RETRYING → HEDGED, which the transition table did not allow, and the replay stopped with IllegalHedgeTransition. A late success of an earlier child can cover the exposure while a retry is still waiting out its backoff. The table now allows RETRYING → HEDGED and PARTIALLY_FILLED, and the waiting retry timer is dropped when the chain moves on (no stale timer, no second hedge). A regression test pins it.
  2. Unwind limit rounding. The unwind IOC limit used to round a buy up and a sell down (looser than its band by up to a tick). It now rounds without loosening, as the hedge cap does.
  3. Rate-limit pressure. With the limit lowered to 6 requests per 10 s, hedges are refused above 80% limiter use (the risk-reducing tier). The run still ended flat through an escalation and two unwind orders, but four hedges were refused. Refusals are also followed by new quote attempts (more orders created than in the baseline window), so the lowered limit does not reduce the attempt count. The tier share (80%) is an ASSUMED value and the decision to admit hedges above it is the owner's.
  4. Fill during cancel pending occurs naturally with a longer cancel latency (800 ms) and is booked once and hedged once.
  5. The size-aware hedge cap never left the touch at 1, 5 or 25 SOL: on this capture the Binance touch covered the hedge size every time (8, 11 and 39 hedges, all filled in full, no partial, no zero, no unwind, the absolute limit never bound). The size-spanning, absolute-limit and fallback paths are therefore exercised by the unit tests only, not by any replay; the IOC partial and zero cases (2c) have no natural occurrence in this capture either.
  6. Maker-venue outage, restart inside a replay, fill-record field (new-level vs queued), amend at replay level (no S0 strategy exists), breaker at replay level, and sampled p99 / p99.9 latency are not built; each is listed in the matrix with its reason. The unit-level evidence for restart, amend, breaker alerts and late-success reconciliation exists as stage-trace files in ems_cases/.
